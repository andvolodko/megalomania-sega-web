🎮 Control Scheme

Left Click: Sega Button C • Select sector, assign men, launch attack, build structures.

Right Click: Sega Button B • Cancel selection, recall units, reduce workers, close window.

Middle Click: Sega Button A • Inspect sector stats, tech details, weapon status.

Mouse Movement: Steers the in-game cursor using proportional velocity tracking.

Pointer Lock: Keeps cursor locked inside the screen so it never slips away during intense battles.

Keyboard Alternatives: Arrow keys to move, C = Action, X = Cancel, Z = Inspect, Space/Enter = Start.

👑 Mega-Lo-Mania: The God Game Guide

You play as one of four gods (Scarlet, Caesar, Oberon, Madcap) battling across 10 Epochs (from 28,000 BC Stone Age to 2001+ AD Nuclear/Space Age) to conquer 28 islands.

Core Strategy Loop:

  • Population: Always leave at least 1 person in your tower so your civilization multiplies!
  • Design: Put men on Design to invent shields, weapons, catapults, and planes.
  • Mining: Put men to mine elements (Solarium, Terrarium, etc.) needed to build inventions.
  • Factory: Forge weapons and build tower shields to prevent premature annihilation.
  • Attack: Form armies and march on enemy towers to claim their sectors and people!
